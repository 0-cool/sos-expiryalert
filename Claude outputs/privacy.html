Who we are

SOS – ExpiryAlert ("the app") is published by SOS Company Apps, operated by Juan Francisco Rojas Beltre, an individual developer based in the Dominican Republic ("we", "us"). Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What the app does

The app helps you remember when your important documents (passports, IDs, driver's licenses, visas and similar) expire, and reminds you before they do.

Information we collect

Account information

Your email address and a password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider as a secure hash; we never see them in plain text.

Document information you enter

For each document: the document type (for example "Passport"), a name you choose (usually the owner's name), the expiration date, optional notes and your reminder preferences.

We never ask for or store document numbers (passport, ID, license, visa or social security numbers) and we never store photos or scans of your documents. Please don't type identification numbers in names or notes.

Purchase information

If you subscribe, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive the subscription status (plan, start and expiration dates, renewal state) through RevenueCat, linked to a random account identifier. We never receive your card or payment details.

Camera and microphone (optional, on-device)

  • Scan document: the camera captures the document and text recognition runs on your device. The temporary image is deleted right after recognition and is never uploaded or saved.
  • Dictate document: your device's speech recognizer processes your voice on your device. Audio and transcripts are never uploaded or saved by the app.

In both cases you review the suggested type, name and date, and only the fields you save are stored.

Stored only on your device

Language, theme and reminder-time preferences, and an offline copy of your document list. Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device.

What we don't collect

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no location, no contacts, no photo library access. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and send account emails (verification codes and password resets).
  • To show your documents on your devices and schedule your reminders.
  • To check whether you have an active subscription.

Service providers

We use these providers only to run the app. They process data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase – account authentication and database (account and document information).
  • RevenueCat – subscription status (random account identifier and purchase records).
  • Google Play – app distribution and payment processing, under Google's own privacy policy.

All information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access rules ensure that only you can read your documents.

How long we keep information

  • While your account is active, we keep your account and documents.
  • When you delete a document, it is removed immediately.
  • When you delete your account, your account and all documents are deleted immediately from our database. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Purchase records may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required by accounting and tax laws.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any document at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Turn reminders on or off in Settings → Notifications, or in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/GDPR, Brazil/LGPD, California/CCPA or the Dominican Republic/Law 172-13), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to processing. Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
